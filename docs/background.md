# Motivation and theory

The page below is the project's ONBOARDING.md, included verbatim. It covers
why Brownian motion on curved surfaces is worth studying, the differential
geometry needed to define it, the generator convention this project commits
to, and the connections to diffusion models and quantitative finance.

Two companion documents go deeper on specific pieces:

- [Differential geometry curriculum](writeups/1-curriculum-checklist.md), a
  topic-by-topic checklist of the background material.
- [Poincare disk derivation](writeups/2-poincare-disk-derivation.md), which
  derives the hyperbolic SDE from this project's own conventions.
